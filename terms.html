Jarvis Studio — Terms of Service

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Jarvis Studio ("the app") is a personal desktop assistant operated by its owner for their own use. It creates short story videos on the owner's computer and, with the owner's permission, posts them to the owner's own TikTok account.

Use of the app

  • The app is for personal use by its owner only. It is not offered to the public.
  • The app connects to TikTok only after the owner signs in and approves access on TikTok's own authorization page. Access can be removed at any time from the app's settings or from TikTok's settings.
  • Content posted through the app must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. AI-generated videos are labelled as AI-generated.

Content

Videos are created from original scripts, AI narration and freely licensed (public-domain or Creative Commons) images. Image credits are included in captions where the licence requires it.
